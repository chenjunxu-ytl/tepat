About

Tepat answers one question: does this word exist? It highlights suspicious words for human investigation — it never judges correctness automatically.

Red marks: structural rule hits. Amber: needs context. Blue: uncertain forms. Hover a highlight for notes; use the buttons in each result for local evidence, PRPM lookup or a web search.

Absence from a dictionary or corpus does not establish a spelling error. An attested word does not certify grammar, meaning or factuality.
