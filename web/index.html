Word flags (PRPM)

Decisions land in word-overrides.json (the source of truth for word-level rules) and take effect on every lookup immediately. Accept writes the override with your definition and closes the issue; the word's final status follows the reporter's complaint. Reject = close without an override.

⟳

Grammar flags (Rule Book)

Flags on Rule Book rule hits — wrong regex, missing context, over-triggering. Open = awaiting review; closed = handled (rule fixed or rejected).

General flags

Page-level subjective reports from the extension popup flag — false alarms, missed problems, suggestions. Not tied to a specific word or rule; handle on GitHub.

About

Tepat answers one question: does this word exist? It highlights suspicious words for human investigation — it never judges correctness automatically.

Red marks: structural rule hits. Amber: needs context. Blue: uncertain forms. Hover a highlight for notes; use the buttons in each result for local evidence, PRPM lookup or a web search.

Absence from a dictionary or corpus does not establish a spelling error. An attested word does not certify grammar, meaning or factuality.
